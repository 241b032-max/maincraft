# Dashboard specification

## Audience

- Management: operational monitoring and weekly performance review.
- Executives: strategic sales, profit and growth decisions.

## Core KPIs

| KPI | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| Sales | Sum of sales amount for the selected period |
| Profit | Sales less cost (or the approved profit field) |
| Profit margin | Profit divided by sales |
| Orders | Distinct order count |
| Average order value | Sales divided by orders |
| Sales growth | Percentage change versus comparable prior period |

## Recommended pages

1. **Management overview:** KPI cards, sales trend, category and region comparisons, key filters.
2. **Performance diagnostics:** product/customer contribution, profitability, variance and growth drivers.
3. **Executive summary:** headline KPIs, period comparison, exceptions, actions and concise business narrative.
